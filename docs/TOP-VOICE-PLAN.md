## What changes in the content

The current batch introduces actual product artifacts: Notion column permissions, Linear stacked pull requests and Penpot Inspect. The images in the [current design collection](../resources/linkedin/current-design/README.md) come from the publishers' own materials. Captions should discuss visible or documented choices and attribute release claims. They must distinguish the shipped product from Rupesh's interpretation or proposed alternative. They must not claim he tested a tool or observed customer results without evidence. The October 18 evening rewrite discusses Framer's October 1 country routing update as an earlier-this-month release, not something launched today.

Those are selected content changes, not a claim that the new batch has already been scheduled. Publication receipts in the normal post state establish scheduling and delivery.

The [official source reader](../automation/design_news.py) checks Linear, Penpot, NNGroup and Smashing Magazine feeds once per local day. It accepts dated entries from the publishers themselves no more than 14 days old, checks freshness against the intended publication time, avoids recently used articles, and adds the source to the writing context as external data. A current-design slot without a usable fresh source is deferred. A limited excerpt supports a limited observation, not an invented product walkthrough. These feeds are an ongoing discovery source; manually checked publishers in this batch are separately sourced.

The [comment context check](../automation/engagement.py) holds observed promotional offers, thin external teasers, media headlines and insufficient context before drafting. The [writing rules](../lib/voice.py) reject recurring stock advice phrases and tell the writer to respond to the detail actually supplied. A particular appreciation, disagreement or answer can be enough. Every response does not need a lesson, trade-off, proposed experiment or closing question. These checks enforce observable preferences and context boundaries; they do not establish human authorship or guarantee good taste.

For each substantial post, record the artifact, exact source URL, publication or capture date, one visible decision, Rupesh's considered position and any limitation. Use the format that carries the point: a tightly cropped real screenshot, a few annotated views, a short text observation or a working resource. A carousel needs progression. A free resource needs a real use and a direct download, rather than a request to comment for access.

## What LinkedIn actually requires

The [current Top Voices help page](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a776208), checked October 18, describes a global, invitation-only blue badge program. It lists consistent platform presence, originality and authentic voice, subject expertise, safety and professionalism. Industry news, commentary and analysis explicitly count as expertise. Editors may also recognise outsized influence in a local market, economy or industry.

The same page recommends useful non-promotional contributions, varied topics and formats, and meaningful follow-up with commenters. It does not publish a required follower count, engagement score, posting frequency, comment quota, paid subscription or guaranteed timetable. LinkedIn may use factors beyond its public list.

Badges are assessed and renewed twice per year. A newly invited member stays a Top Voice for at least six months and can be renewed while meeting requirements. LinkedIn can add or remove members outside the usual periods and remove status for policy violations.

The [gold Community Top Voice badge](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6245087) was retired on October 8, 2024. The last badges expired by December 7, 2024. Contributing to collaborative articles does not now earn a gold badge or provide an automatic route to blue. An older badge-display help page still mentions both programs; the explicit retirement notice governs this distinction.

LinkedIn's [content recommendation guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/blog/content-recommendations-avoid) discourages copied posts without added insight, promotional comments, engagement bait and engagement pods. Its [community policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies) require authentic responses and prohibit artificial engagement and misleading professional claims. The [User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement) prohibits bots and unauthorized methods to create, comment on, like or share posts. The [automated activity guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567) also addresses website automation and scraping. Use only permitted provider access; a connection or paid plan alone is not proof that every activity is authorized. These rules do not imply that every authorized publishing API is prohibited.

## Prepare one credible nomination

The help page links to the active [official nomination form](https://forms.office.com/r/edJuW8KjA3). It accepts self-nominations and nominations of others on a rolling basis. LinkedIn says it reviews nominations quarterly. The form states that nomination does not guarantee selection, a response or individual feedback. Multiple nominations do not increase the chance of selection, and self-nominations should be limited to one every six months.

Before submitting:

- Choose one or two genuinely strong published LinkedIn posts from the past month. Supply direct post URLs, not scheduled drafts, image previews or a promise of future work.
- Explain concrete expertise and usefulness in no more than 300 words. Include real impact only when supported; views, comments, clients, hiring outcomes and endorsements must not be invented.
- Use the confirmed full name and [profile URL](https://www.linkedin.com/in/therupeshkumar). Use the Self relationship option. Confirm the actual professional category and geographic base if they have not been established; the form explicitly offers India and other regions, but the client's timezone alone does not prove residence.
- Check prior self-nomination history. An unknown history is not proof that six months have passed. Email and a separate nominator's identity are optional, so do not invent them.
- Acknowledge the form's terms and submit once only when the evidence and interval support it. Preserve the real acknowledgement without claiming editorial acceptance. No nomination was submitted by this document.
