## About

I work across creative direction, product design, startup founding, and brand strategy. I am building a consulting practice and exploring founding designer roles with teams that want design to contribute to the decisions behind their product.

My focus is the connection between what a company promises and what a customer actually experiences. A useful product, a clear brand, and an understandable interface need to support the same direction. I am interested in helping early-stage teams make those choices together.

That can mean clarifying the problem a product should solve, examining an important customer task, or deciding which parts of an experience deserve attention first. It can also mean connecting a brand's positioning to the language and behavior of the product.

For a founding designer role, I am interested in the questions that come before a finished screen: who the product is for, what the team needs to learn, and how design should work alongside the founders and engineering team.

Here I share practical thinking on product decisions, creative direction, brand strategy, and the design foundations of early-stage companies. I want those posts to lead to useful exchanges with founders, product leaders, and other designers.

If you are considering design consulting or a founding designer hire, I would welcome a conversation about your team's stage, the problem you are working through, and the role you want design to play.
